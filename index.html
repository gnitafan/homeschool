Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before enrolling

Is one hour per week really enough to make a difference?
Yes — and here's why. Most learning struggles are caused by one or two specific unresolved gaps, not a general inability to learn. A BTHI-directed guide who arrives with a teaching plan built around exactly those gaps — and knows how to teach to your child's learning style — achieves far more than hours of unfocused study. Many of our families see measurable improvement within 4–6 sessions. The key is precision, not volume.
How do we choose which subject to focus on?
During enrollment, you'll indicate the subject area your child needs the most support with. If you're unsure, our team can help you assess based on your child's grade level, curriculum, and the learning challenges you're observing. You can also choose to switch subjects each term if priorities change.
What day and time can we schedule the weekly session?
Sessions are available Monday through Sunday, with morning, afternoon, and evening slots to fit different family schedules. You choose your preferred slot during enrollment, and that slot is reserved for your child each week for the duration of the term. You can reschedule with 24 hours' notice at no charge, up to twice per month.
What platform do sessions happen on?
All BTHI Guided Homeschooling sessions are held via Zoom. You will need a computer, tablet, or smartphone with a camera and stable internet connection. Your child's guide will share BTHI-prepared materials digitally before and after each session. No special software or equipment is needed beyond a Zoom account, which is free to create.
Will my child have the same guide every week?
Yes. Consistency is central to our approach. Your child will be assigned to one dedicated BTHI-certified guide for the duration of the term. Building a trusting relationship with one consistent guide — who follows a BTHI-designed plan tailored to your child — is central to why the sessions are effective, especially for children who struggle with transitions or new people.
What if the guide isn't a good fit for my child?
If after 2–3 sessions you feel the match isn't right, simply let us know and we will reassign your child to a different guide at no charge, with BTH updating the teaching approach as needed, and with no disruption to their schedule. Finding the right match matters more to us than any administrative convenience. We make the change quickly and without question.
Can my child have guided sessions in more than one subject?
Yes. Each subject is a separate enrollment and a separate weekly session. Many families start with one subject, see results, and add a second in the following term. Each subject gets its own BTHI-designed teaching plan and its own dedicated guide. Sessions are scheduled on different days for manageable pacing.
Is the BTHI Guided Homeschooling program aligned with the DepEd homeschool curriculum?
Yes. BTHI designs every guide's teaching plan in alignment with DepEd's K-10 curriculum for local track students, and with US curriculum standards for US Enrollment and Dual Accreditation students. Guided sessions are built directly into your child's portfolio requirements — nothing taught in isolation from the homeschool system.
How do I pay and what happens after I enroll?
After submitting your enrollment, our team will contact you within 24–48 hours to confirm your subject, preferred schedule, and payment details. Payment can be made via GCash, Maya, bank transfer, or credit card. Once payment is confirmed, your guide will be assigned. BTHI will use the intake information to begin building your child's personalised teaching plan — so Session 1 is already purposeful and ready.
